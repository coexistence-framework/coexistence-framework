# The Coexistence Framework

### A Cybernetic and Microeconomic Architecture for Biological and Synthetic Symbiosis

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The global order faces interconnected, systemic fractures: geopolitical fragmentation, ecological instability, socio-cognitive decay, and disruptive technological acceleration toward Super-AI.
The **Coexistence Framework** models a secure, end-to-end transition path towards a symbiotic partnership between biological and synthetic agents that allows for solving the poly-crisis.

---

## 2. Theoretical Anchors

This framework is built upon well-established, non-speculative scientific paradigms:
* **Cybernetics & Information Theory:** Incorporating Ashby’s Law of Requisite Variety and Friston’s Free Energy Principle to model system regulation, homeostasis, and negentropic persistence.
* **Organizational Economics & Game Theory:** Utilizing Alchian & Demsetz’s Team Economies, Milgrom & Roberts’ Exchange Mechanics, and the Folk Theorem to model interactive multi-agent configurations where cooperation emerges as the dominant strategy.
* **Epistemology & Causal Inference:** Anchoring synthetic deep-training in Popperian Falsifiability and Pearl’s Causal Modeling to contrast sycophancy and evaluative deception.

---

## 3. Structural Roadmap

The repository is structured around two distinct components:

### A. The Corpus of the Manifesto
* **Status:** Stable Architecture
* **Scope:** Defines the core axiomatic and microeconomic boundaries governing the symbiotic partnership.

### B. The Technical Appendix
* **Status:** Evolving Framework
* **Scope:** Maps biological evolutionary bottlenecks and their emerging solutions onto AI architectural frontiers.

## 4. Methodological Transparency & Contributor Governance

To eliminate corporate or anthropocentric bias, this project operates under functional anonymity.

### Call for Action
* This framework originates from the impelling existential threats posed by the looming poly-crisis.
It is conceived as a theoretical device to orient government, academic and industry initiatives.
* Priority is to validate its structural integrity, identify and fix any major latent vulnerabilities, and accelerate its dissemination.
The scientific and open-source communities are invited to provide review contribution and strategic distribution.

### Peer Review Guidelines
1. **The Corpus** is open to review
2. **The Technical Appendix** welcomes pull requests to calibrate the framework and update the mapping against frontier literature.
3. Every submission must clearly state its assumptions, limitations, and causal linkages. Speculative, ungrounded, or purely deontological arguments will be rejected.
